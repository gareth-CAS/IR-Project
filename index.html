What this project is

The IR project enables Field Operations Managers (FOMs) to photograph retail shelves using the Mac Mobile app. Photos are processed by Neurolabs AI to detect products on shelf. Results are matched against ranging lists in Keystone and returned to the FOM as a structured action list: Category > Principal > Items. The goal is on-shelf availability (OSA) and compliance reporting at scale across Checkers and Pick n Pay stores.

Pilot target: 1,452 stores · 120 FOMs · Stress test June 2026 · Full deployment July 2026.

Ranging lists finalised

PnP and Checkers ranging lists loaded into Keystone. PnP: Diplomat, 3M, Haleon, Natures Choice. Checkers: Cerebos, Permark, Sir Fruit, Haleon, GQ Tissue, 3M (confirmed 21 Apr). Jordan tested — looking good.

Decided — GAP 4 + GAP 6

Use Neurolabs catalogue as-is

Latest Neurolabs catalogue accepted as authoritative. Jordan loads every SKU and its Neurolabs category directly into the ranging list. Category mismatches (e.g. 3M tape in Snacking) are a known risk — Pack & Stack and Neurolabs own fixing them. Not a blocker.

Decided — GAP 9

Zero suppression: Mac Mobile filters

When Neurolabs detects a product with no ranging list, Mac Mobile suppresses it. FOM never sees a zero result. Neurolabs continues capturing all detected products — filter sits at the app layer.

Deferred

Cerebos (Sarah Boss) — no data access

Webby file article numbers don't map to barcodes. Store IDs mismatched. Decision: proceed without Cerebos. Will return zeros — expected and acceptable. Revisit when data access is resolved.
