What this project is

The IR project enables Field Operations Managers (FOMs) to photograph retail shelves using the Mac Mobile app. Photos are processed by Neurolabs AI to detect products on shelf. Results are matched against ranging lists in Keystone and returned to the FOM as a structured action list: Principal > Category > Items. The goal is on-shelf availability (OSA) and compliance reporting at scale across Checkers and Pick n Pay stores.

80 stores · 8 FOMs active · Scale up May/June 2026 · Stress test 1 July 2026 (1,400+ stores, ~100 FOMs)
